RumiTX 2030 - Futuristic Interface Design Prompt

Design a 2030-era holographic radio interface for the RumiTX DMR app on the DOOGEE S80. This is a replacement for the stock Talkie app, used by amateur radio operators and professionals.

DESIGN LANGUAGE
- Dark holographic: deep blacks (#050508) with cyan (#00F0FF) and amber (#FFB000) accents
- Glowing elements: frequency display, signal meters, and PTT button all emit soft glow
- Glassmorphism: semi-transparent panels with blur, floating over a subtle animated background
- No flat Material Design - everything has depth, glow, and motion

MAIN SCREEN LAYOUT
Top: Massive glowing frequency display (e.g., "446.5555 MHz") in a futuristic font, with a subtle scanline effect. Below it, smaller text shows channel name, mode (DMR/Analog), and signal strength as an animated bar.

Middle: Real-time spectrum visualization - a scrolling waterfall or bar spectrum showing RF activity around the tuned frequency. When squelch opens, the active signal glows brighter.

Bottom: Large circular PTT button, centrally positioned. Idle: dim cyan ring. Pressed: bright orange glow with expanding ripple animation and "TRANSMITTING" text. The button should feel tactile and satisfying.

Side: Vertical channel list with holographic cards. Each card shows frequency, name, and a mini signal indicator. Swipe to switch.

DISCOVERY MODE
Full-screen spectrum scanner with:
- Live frequency readout counting up rapidly during scan
- Progress bar with glowing fill
- Found signals pop in as glowing markers on the spectrum
- Tap a marker to instantly create a channel
- Signal strength shown as vertical bars

COLOR CODING
- Cyan (#00F0FF): RX audio, idle state, information
- Orange (#FF6B00): TX active, warnings
- Green (#00FF88): Signal locked, success
- Red (#FF2244): Errors, squelch closed
- Amber (#FFB000): Channel names, highlights

ANIMATIONS (2030 standard)
- PTT press: 150ms scale-up + glow intensify + ripple
- Channel switch: frequency display does a quick roll/odometer effect
- Squelch open: spectrum bars animate upward, audio wave icon pulses
- Scan: smooth frequency counter, not jerky steps
- All transitions: 200-300ms, ease-out cubic

TYPOGRAPHY
- Frequency: monospace/futuristic (like "Orbitron" or "Rajdhani"), large, glowing
- Labels: clean sans-serif, uppercase, letter-spaced
- Numbers: tabular figures for stable display

DARK MODE ONLY
This is a radio used in the field, often at night. Pure blacks, no white backgrounds. OLED-friendly.

ACCESSIBILITY
- Large touch targets (min 48dp, PTT 96dp)
- High contrast ratios for outdoor sunlight
- Haptic feedback on PTT press/release and channel change

DO NOT INCLUDE
- Skeuomorphic radio knobs or vintage styling
- Cluttered menus - keep it to one main screen + discovery
- iOS-style flat design
- Anything that looks like a 2020 app

The goal: someone in 2030 picks up this phone and thinks "this is what a radio should look like."
