RumiTX 2030 - HIDL regression after reboot. Need your analysis.

- Now:
  - HIDL getService() returns the service ("HIDL service FOUND")
  - BUT no speaker chirp
  - PTT fails ("PTT FAILED")
  - Channel programming fails ("failed to program channel")

WHAT I TRIED
- Added explicit transact() calls for talkie_init (txn 1) and talkie_app_start (txn 3) via IHwBinder reflection
- This did NOT fix it - still no chirp
- Reverted to the simple getService() test that worked before
- Still no chirp after revert

KEY QUESTION
Why did getService() alone trigger the speaker chirp before the reboot, but not after?
Was the chirp actually coming from our HIDL call, or from something else (daemon APP_START, module state, stock service reacting)?

OTHER SYMPTOMS
- PTT fails even though the daemon shows READY
- Channel programming fails even though /dev/ttyMT1 is 666
- This suggests the daemon might not have proper module ownership, OR the module needs a different init sequence after reboot

HYPOTHESES
1. The daemon needs to send APP_START (0x6901) via ioctl before HIDL speaker init works - and it's not doing that on restart
2. The module is in a different state after reboot and needs a full init sequence
